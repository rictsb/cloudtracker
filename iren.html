what $1 of ARR is worth
Costs are tiny. Power at $35/MWh is 1.8% of revenue — IREN owns the substations. Gross margin ~94%; 65% EBITDA after overhead.10,512 MWh per IT MW-yr at PUE 1.2
Capex ~$36m per IT MW, corroborated three ways: balance-sheet GPU fleet, rack build-up, and the FY27 guide ÷ 2028 build.$27.5bn ÷ 762 IT MW = $36.1m
Maintenance, not replacement. The refresh is a tray swap — racks, liquid loop and power stay — at ~55% of GPU cost every four years, plus 3%/yr on failures and spares: $4.7m per MW-yr, the same cash as a full six-year replace. Never refreshing (3.03×) sits under the base (3.91×). Book life: IREN 5 yr; CoreWeave, MSFT, GOOG 6.10-K Note 2 · peer 10-Ks

Some equity, not none. The flywheel could cover the build, but the company raised 16% of last year’s funding in equity and keeps the ATM live.
