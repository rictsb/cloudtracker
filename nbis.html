what $1 of ARR is worth
Margins are still climbing. AI-cloud adjusted EBITDA margin 19% (Q3-25) → 24% → 45% → 50% (Q2-26) as co-location and lease costs fall as a share of revenue; group guide ~40% for 2026. Owned campuses at Finland-style PUE 1.13 should take it toward the owner-operator 65%.quarterly 6-Ks · Q4-25 call
Capex is $30m of GPUs per MW plus $8m of shell on owned sites. Management’s split: land and power ~1%, data-centre build 18–20%, GPUs ~80%; 2026 guide $20–25bn (raised from $16–20bn in May); H1-26 spent $8.1bn.Q3-25 call · Q1-26 and Q2-26 letters
Payback 1 year 10 months on Q2-26 deals (was 2–3 years), on prepayments of 50–60% of capex. Maintenance as a tray swap at 55% of GPU cost every four years plus 3%/yr spares: $5.0m per MW-yr on $30m of silicon.Q2-26 letter · model

Valuation

EV / ARR today
Some equity, not none. $6.9bn of equity-linked capital in 2026 (NVIDIA warrant, ATM) against $20–25bn of capex. Base: 10% of the 2027–30 build plus the residual the flywheel cannot cover, at an assumed $250 — the bridge shows the count.6-Ks · 12 Aug letter
The multiple is the swing. At $20m per MW and 55% EBITDA, $1 of run-rate is worth ~3.3× — between CoreWeave’s tenant 2.75× and IREN’s owner-operator 4.06×, because Nebius owns most of its shell but pays more for silicon per dollar of revenue on shorter contracts. Signed contracts never escalate; they reprice when they end.contracted 2-yr at 10.5% · short-term at 13%
