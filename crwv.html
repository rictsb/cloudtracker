Success-based capex. Contracted revenue more than covers capex within the contract term; each deployment is financed by a self-amortising delayed-draw term loan plus prepayments. DDTL 4.0 ($8.5bn, SOFR+225 / 5.9% fixed, non-recourse, A3) advances 90% of cost; DDTL 5.5 ($2.6bn, SOFR+550) finances 3-year contracts.8-Ks 30 Mar, 15 May, 7 Aug 2026
“Weighted-average cost of debt down almost 300 bps in a year.” $35.6bn of debt at 30 June: DDTLs $13.6bn, senior notes $10.0bn at 9–9.75%, converts $6.6bn at 1.75%, OEM financing $4.8bn. Interest ran $640m in Q2 against $1.51bn of adjusted EBITDA.Q2-26 10-Q Note 8 · 11 Aug call
The catch: prepayments are 15–25% of contract value and lenders advance 90% only against signed take-or-pay. Unsigned capacity needs ~$16.5m of equity per MW — or, as in 2025, the NVIDIA backstop and equity placements ($2bn at $87.20, $1bn at $109).424B4 · 8-Ks 23 Jan, 15 Apr 2026

What one megawatt earns

$2.75
what $1 of ARR is worth
Rent is the cost. Adjusted EBITDA margin 57–59% after rent, power and data-centre staff; management says mature contracts run ~70%. Core Scientific charges ~$120/kW-month ($1.4m per MW-yr), Applied Digital ~$150 ($1.8m).Q2-26 release · lessor filings
Capex is $30–33m per active MW and rising. Management capex ÷ active MW added gives $30m for FY25 and $25m for H1-26; component inflation is passed through in contract pricing since Q1-26.10-K FY25 · Q1-26 and Q2-26 calls
Maintenance, not replacement. Refresh as a tray swap at ~55% of GPU cost every four years plus 3%/yr spares: $5.5m per MW-yr on $33m of silicon. A tenant keeps $2m of every $14m today; the multiple is what the market’s +6%/yr does to that over five years.model · peer 10-Ks
