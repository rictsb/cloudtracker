Costs are tiny. Power at $35/MWh is 1.8% of revenue — IREN owns the substations. Gross margin ~94%; 65% EBITDA after overhead.10,512 MWh per IT MW-yr at PUE 1.2
Capex is $44m per IT MW today and rising. $29m of GPUs and ancillaries on the Microsoft/Dell order plus IREN’s own $15m shell; 15–20% more on 2027 orders (CFO), ~$40m of silicon for Rubin, ~$45m after. Pricing is set so IREN’s ~2-year GPU payback holds on each vintage.8-K 3-Nov-25 · Nov-25 deck · FY26 call
Maintenance, not replacement. The refresh is a tray swap — racks, liquid loop and power stay — at ~55% of GPU cost every four years, plus 3%/yr on failures and spares: $5.7m per MW-yr on $34m of silicon. Never refreshing (2.91×) sits well under the base (4.06×) once pricing rises with cost. Book life: IREN 5 yr; CoreWeave, MSFT, GOOG 6.10-K Note 2 · peer 10-Ks

Valuation

EV / ARR today
Some equity, not none. The flywheel could cover the build, but the company raised 16% of last year’s funding in equity and keeps the ATM live. Base: 10% of the 2027–30 build plus the residual the flywheel cannot cover, $14.6bn, at an assumed $55 — +265m shares, 757m diluted once the converts are counted. At today’s price the same raise costs ~390m shares and about $22 a share.FY26 call · 10-K Note 24
The regime is the swing, not the ARR. Same $62.3bn, same balance sheet: $95 if the fleet is never refreshed, $98 at flat $/MW, $156 with pricing that keeps IREN’s two-year GPU payback on rising silicon cost for five years, then flat. Signed contracts never escalate; they reprice when they end.contracted 3-yr at 10.5% · spot at 13%
